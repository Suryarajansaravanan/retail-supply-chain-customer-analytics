# retail-supply-chain-customer-analytics
End-to-End Retail Supply Chain &amp; Customer Analytics Platform using PostgreSQL and Power BI

## Executive Summary

This project was developed to transform raw retail transaction data into a business intelligence solution using PostgreSQL and Power BI.

The objective was to identify operational inefficiencies, improve profitability, optimize delivery performance, and generate actionable business insights from retail sales, customer, product, and supply chain data.

The final solution provides management with a centralized dashboard for monitoring performance and supporting data-driven decision-making.

---

# Business Problem

The retail company was experiencing several business challenges:

### Sales Challenges

* Inconsistent regional sales performance
* Limited visibility into profitable products and categories
* Lack of centralized KPI monitoring

### Customer Challenges

* Difficulty identifying high-value customers
* Limited customer segmentation analysis
* Poor visibility into customer profitability

### Product Challenges

* Low-profit and loss-making products
* High discount dependency reducing profitability
* Limited understanding of product performance

### Supply Chain Challenges

* Delayed deliveries affecting customer satisfaction
* Lack of logistics performance monitoring
* No centralized operational reporting system

Without a structured analytics solution, management could not effectively identify performance gaps or improvement opportunities.

---

# Project Objectives

The project was designed to:

* Analyze sales and profitability performance
* Identify top-performing regions and products
* Detect loss-making products
* Evaluate customer purchasing behavior
* Monitor delivery performance
* Build an interactive Power BI dashboard
* Generate business recommendations supported by data

---

## Sales Insights

### Findings

* The company generated total sales of **66.93K**.
* Total profit reached **17.82K**.
* Overall profit margin achieved **26.62%**.
* Sales showed consistent growth from **2011** to **2014**.
* Technology products generated the highest profitability.
* Revenue was concentrated within a limited number of high-performing regions.

### Business Impact

The sales analysis provided visibility into revenue growth patterns and highlighted opportunities for regional expansion.

---

## Customer Insights

### Findings

* A total of **181 customers** were analyzed.
* High-value customers contributed a significant portion of overall revenue.
* Corporate customers demonstrated strong profitability.
* Customer segmentation revealed different purchasing behaviors across groups.

### Business Impact

The customer analysis supports targeted retention strategies and improved customer lifetime value.

---

## Product Insights

### Findings

* Several products generated negative profits despite producing sales.
* Excessive discounting reduced overall profitability.
* Office Supplies generated high sales volume but lower margins.
* A small number of products contributed a significant share of revenue.

### Business Impact

The analysis identified opportunities to improve pricing strategies and increase profitability.

---

## Supply Chain Insights

### Findings

* Average delivery duration was **3.68 Days**.
* Delivery performance varied across regions.
* Some regions experienced longer delivery times than others.
* Logistics efficiency directly influenced customer experience.

### Business Impact

Reducing delivery times can improve operational efficiency and customer satisfaction.

---

# Business Recommendations

## 1. Reduce Excessive Discounting

Several products generated negative profits due to aggressive discount strategies.

### Expected Outcome

* Improve profit margin beyond **26.62%**
* Increase overall profitability
* Reduce revenue leakage

---

## 2. Optimize Delivery Operations

Focus logistics improvements on regions experiencing longer delivery times.

### Expected Outcome

* Reduce average delivery time below **3.68 Days**
* Improve customer satisfaction
* Increase operational efficiency

---

## 3. Focus on High-Value Customers

Develop retention programs for top-performing customer segments.

### Expected Outcome

* Increase repeat purchases
* Improve customer lifetime value
* Generate stable revenue growth

---

## 4. Improve Inventory Planning

Maintain inventory availability for high-demand products.

### Expected Outcome

* Reduce stock shortages
* Improve order fulfillment rates
* Increase sales opportunities

---

## 5. Review Loss-Making Products

Reprice, optimize, or discontinue consistently unprofitable products.

### Expected Outcome

* Improve profitability
* Optimize resource allocation
* Strengthen product portfolio performance
